Short version: VERITY has no server, no database and no accounts, so there is very little it could collect. Here is exactly what happens with your data.

What VERITY stores about you

  • Nothing on any server. VERITY is a static website. It has no backend, no database and no user accounts, and it never receives your name, email or wallet details.
  • On your own device only: your display name, your theme choice, which wallet you picked, and a cache of polls so pages load faster. All of it lives in your browser's local storage. "Sign out and forget my name" clears the personal parts, and clearing your browser data removes everything.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no trackers. There are no advertising or analytics scripts on this site.

What goes on the blockchain

When you vote or create a poll, your wallet sends a transaction to the Ethereum Sepolia network. That transaction is public and permanent. It records your wallet address, the poll, and the option you chose (or the poll you created). Anyone can see it on Etherscan, and it can never be edited or deleted, by you, by VERITY or by anyone else. Your wallet address is not your name, but if you share your address, or use the same one elsewhere, people may be able to link it to you. Please don't vote on VERITY if you need your choice to stay secret.

Your choices

  • You can browse every poll and recount any result without connecting a wallet. Only voting and creating need one.
  • You can use a fresh wallet address just for VERITY if you don't want it linked to your other activity.
  • You can remove everything VERITY stored on your device from Settings or by clearing your browser data.
