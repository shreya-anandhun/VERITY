The plain-language rules for using VERITY. Reading time: about two minutes.

1. What you're using

VERITY is a free, open-source student project that lets you create polls and vote on them through a smart contract on the Ethereum Sepolia test network. It is provided as-is, for learning and for small-community votes. It is not a certified election system and must not be used for anything where a wrong or disputed result could cause real harm.

2. Everything is permanent and public

  • Every poll and vote is written to a public blockchain. It cannot be edited or deleted, by you or by VERITY. Think before you post.
  • Your votes are linked to your wallet address, which anyone can see.
  • If the website goes away, your polls and votes stay on the blockchain and remain readable on Etherscan.

3. What you agree not to do

  • Create polls that are abusive, harassing, hateful, sexual, illegal, or that target a private person.
  • Create polls that impersonate someone else or that are designed to mislead.
  • Use multiple wallets to vote more than once in a poll where the organiser asked for one vote per person.
  • Attack, overload or try to interfere with the website, the contract or the network.

4. Reported polls

Because polls can never be deleted from the blockchain, VERITY's only tool is to stop showing a poll on this website. If a poll breaks rule 3, report it with its number. A hidden poll still exists on-chain and can still be read directly from the contract; it simply no longer appears on VERITY. The hidden list is published in the site's source code, so hiding is itself transparent.

5. No fees, no money, no warranty

  • VERITY charges nothing and never holds anyone's funds. The only cost is the Sepolia network fee, paid in free test ETH with no real-world value.
  • VERITY never asks for your keys, seed phrase or password. Anyone who does is not VERITY.
  • The site is provided without warranty of any kind. It may be unavailable, buggy or discontinued at any time, and its maintainer is not liable for any loss arising from its use.

6. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated as the project grows; the date below changes when they do. Questions go to the contact form or verity.helpdesk@gmail.com.
