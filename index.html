Why trust VERITY

You don't have to. You can check.

Votes live on-chain, not in a database

Each vote is an Ethereum transaction, copied across thousands of computers. There is no VERITY server to hack or quietly edit.

Anyone can verify any vote

Every vote has a public receipt on Etherscan, and Recount re-adds a whole poll in your own browser.

No admin. No edit button.

The contract has no owner and can't be upgraded. Nobody can change or delete a vote. A poll's creator can only close it early, which stops new votes.

Your keys stay yours

Your wallet signs everything in its own window. VERITY never sees your keys or holds your money, and charges no fees.

Questions

The honest answers

Do I need a wallet?

To vote or create a poll, yes: Phantom or MetaMask, both free. You can browse every poll, see results of ended polls and recount them without one.

Which network is it on? Does it cost money?

Sepolia, Ethereum's test network. Each vote costs a tiny network fee paid in free test ETH, so it costs you nothing real. VERITY itself charges no fees.

Are votes anonymous?

Not fully. Votes are linked to a wallet address, not your name, but anyone can see which address picked which option. That openness is what makes them verifiable.

How do I verify a result?

Press Recount on any poll to re-add every vote straight from the blockchain, or open the contract on Etherscan and call getPoll yourself.

What happens when a poll ends?

Voting stops automatically at the end time, and the final result is shown to everyone. It stays on the blockchain permanently.

Is there an admin who can change things?

No. The contract has no owner, no upgrade switch and no function that edits votes. The only power anyone has is closing a poll they created themselves.
