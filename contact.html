Reporting a poll

Polls can never be deleted from the blockchain, so what VERITY can do is stop showing one on this website. Pick "Reporting a poll" above, give the poll number and say what's wrong. Hidden polls are listed openly in the site's source code ↗, so moderation is as transparent as the votes. The rules are on the Terms page.
