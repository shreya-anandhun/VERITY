The short version

One wallet, one vote, written where nobody can edit it.

VERITY has no accounts and no passwords. Your wallet is your identity, and every vote is a transaction on the Ethereum Sepolia network: public, permanent and checkable by anyone.

  1. 01

    Connect your wallet

    Click Connect Wallet. Your wallet (Phantom, or MetaMask) opens its own window and asks you to approve. VERITY only ever sees your public address, never your password or keys.

  2. 02

    Pick a live poll

    Open Live Polls, tap a question and choose an option. Polls stay open until their end time, or until the creator closes them.

  3. 03

    Confirm and sign

    VERITY asks "are you sure?", then your wallet asks you to sign the transaction. Signing proves the vote came from your wallet. A tiny network fee (test ETH, worth nothing) is paid.

  4. 04

    Recorded on the blockchain

    The vote goes to VERITY's smart contract on Sepolia. The contract checks you haven't voted in this poll before, then adds your vote to a block. Once there, nobody can change or delete it, including us.

  5. 05

    Verify it, anywhere

    Every vote gets a transaction hash. Paste it on Transactions or open it on Etherscan to see exactly what was recorded, from any computer, without trusting VERITY.

VERITY's website only displays what's on the blockchain. It can't add, hide or change a vote, which is why two people on two computers always see the same numbers.

Quick answers

Why can I only vote once?

The contract keeps a list of which wallets have voted in each poll. A second vote from the same wallet is rejected before it's recorded, so it shows up as Failed on Transactions.

Is my vote anonymous?

Your wallet address is public with your vote, but nothing links that address to your name unless you share it. VERITY never asks for personal details; your display name stays in your own browser.

What is the "network fee"?

Every blockchain transaction costs a little ETH to process. VERITY runs on Sepolia, a test network, so the ETH is free from a faucet and has no real value.

Can a vote be changed or deleted?

No. Once a block is confirmed, changing it would mean rewriting the whole chain across thousands of computers. That permanence is the point.

Why can't I see results before voting?

On purpose. Hiding the tally until you've voted stops people from just joining the crowd. The counts are still public: anyone can read them from the contract on Etherscan at any time, and they appear on VERITY the moment the poll ends.

Could someone vote twice with two wallets?

Yes. "One wallet, one vote" means one wallet, and a person can create several. That's acceptable for club polls among people who know each other; it's not a system for high-stakes elections. A stricter version would check membership before letting a wallet vote.

What if the website goes down?

The votes are still on Sepolia. Anyone can read the contract directly on Etherscan, so the results exist independently of VERITY.
